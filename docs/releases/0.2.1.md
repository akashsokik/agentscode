AgentsCode 0.2.1 adds a SuperAgent that runs your team for you, an integrated browser, and a clearer transcript where teammates, mentions and agents are easy to tell apart.

0.2.1 is the first published build of the 0.2 line: the 0.2.0 tag never produced a release because its build checks failed. It carries everything planned for 0.2.0, plus the two fixes below.

## Highlights

- **SuperAgent.** One read-only agent that runs the team across every project: it watches your agents, works a goals backlog, messages and delegates, and asks you about the rest. Its Decisions, Goals and Routines live in the right panel, where anything it did on its own can be undone. Daily budget, hourly wake-ups, quiet hours and a creation cap only limit what it starts on its own; work you asked for always runs to the end.
- **Quick window.** A floating SuperAgent composer over any app (Shift+Cmd+Space), with your team at a glance and its questions answerable inline.
- **Routines and eyes.** Scheduled routines you approve. Read-only GitHub and CI tools, a read-only check runner (git, tests, typecheck, lint) and app screenshots for the SuperAgent, reviewers and testers.
- **Restart-safe mail.** Queued mail stays queued across a restart, and mail that was mid-delivery goes once more, marked as such.
- **A transcript that reads as one conversation.** A teammate's message opens its turn like yours does, leading with what it means (a report's summary and outcome, a delegation's task). Handoffs are quiet rows that follow the work to its report. Outcomes read as the work: Changes needed, Approved, Passed, Failed, Done, Blocked.
- **@-mentions for agents, projects and files.** One picker in every composer, channels and the quick window included. Mentioning an agent or project tells the receiving agent exactly who or what you mean; in channels it still starts the members you name.
- **Agent avatars.** Every agent gets its own flat, organic avatar with two pill eyes that show its mood (working, waiting on you, done, failed). Pick a shape and colour in Agent Settings; existing agents get a distinct look once. Member stacks in channel headers, and the recipient's avatar on every "X to Y" line.
- **Agent Settings in sections.** General, Soul, Memory and Dreaming, one at a time, with the last section remembered and keyboard navigation.
- **All-projects OrcaCode agents.** An OrcaCode agent can now work across every project instead of one. Needs `orcacode` 0.12.0 or newer.

## Also in this release

- An integrated browser: browser tabs in the desktop app, localhost previews, and browser tools agents use to check web work.
- A shared user profile (user.md, About you) that every agent reads, learned from what you say and reviewed by you.
- Mermaid diagrams in replies, with a floating zoomable viewer; interactive OpenUI replies; walkthroughs of an agent's work.
- Resizable and maximizable panes.

## Fixes

- The quick window follows the app theme: choosing Light or Dark in the app applies to it right away, and System follows macOS live. Before, it kept whatever theme it opened with.
- Release builds: the macOS-only restart script tests are skipped on Linux and Windows, which is what stopped 0.2.0 from publishing.
- Quitting AgentsCode closes the integrated browser's tabs before the windows, so the app quits cleanly.
- `scripts/restart-desktop.sh`: one restart path onto a new build that survives a stuck quit and never leaves the app closed.
- No blank space after closing a split pane's tab; long status lines and composer pickers no longer overflow.
- A stopped dream says why instead of "aborted by user", and closing AgentsCode records no failed dream.

## Requirements

The CLIs you want to use, on your PATH and signed in: `claude` for Claude Code, `codex` for Codex, and `orcacode` 0.10.0 or newer for OrcaCode (0.12.0 or newer for All-projects OrcaCode agents).

- **macOS:** if this build is not signed with a Developer ID, a DMG downloaded in a browser is blocked the first time. Install from Terminal instead, which opens the app directly: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/akashsokik/agentscode/main/install.sh | bash`. To use the DMG, click Done on the warning, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway and enter your password.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the unsigned installer (More info > Run anyway).
